Educational progression

WYLWYL

A sample learning path from early counting to advanced mathematical tools used in modern machine learning.

Counting and patterns

Counting helps humans track quantities, compare sets, and solve practical problems like sharing and keeping score.

Arithmetic and measurement

Basic addition, subtraction, and measurement support tasks like budgeting, cooking, and navigation.

Ratios and early algebra

Understanding relationships, proportions, and simple equations helps model simple systems and reason about change.

Geometry and functions

Geometry and functions describe shapes, motion, and systems; they become useful for engineering and data analysis.

Calculus and statistics

Calculus describes continuous change, and statistics helps make sense of data, both of which are used in training neural networks.

Counting

Problem solved: tracking discrete objects and quantities.

Algebra

Problem solved: finding unknown values and expressing relationships.

Calculus

Problem solved: modeling continuous systems and optimization in machine learning.

Statistics

Problem solved: interpreting uncertain information and improving predictions.
